# Sonar science and quality control for DeepSignal

Build **#170 quality-weighted depth** first. It uses fields a tech already recognizes, can work between crossings, and can say when two disagreeing instruments cannot be separated. Build **#171 refraction residuals** next. A repeat swath can test whether an error in a particular part of the sound-speed profile would explain the curl, giving the watch a reason to examine a cast. Build **#172 sounding uncertainty** alongside it. It estimates vertical and horizontal precision per beam and gives shore processors an independent check at crossings. The first science pilots are #173 for SBP29 reflectors and #174 for water-column flares. All five produce separate, traceable observations.

## Fusion backbone

Each observation carries its footprint, measurement function h, covariance, frame and parent IDs for raw data, clock, calibration and fit. Represent the ancestry as the evidence graph from the drift report. Different renderings of one ping are one observation. Sensor families sharing a clock, draft, sound speed, wake or calibration get a common error term. Unknown covariance means collapse to one representative per family or show a range of results. Give a reference its resolution, offsets in time/space/depth and representativeness uncertainty as in `ReferenceObservation`. A GMRT grid cell incorporating this line cannot validate that line.

For a local time window or crossing tile, model y = h(x) + U a + e, with shared errors a ~ N(0,C), independent e ~ N(0,D), and R = D + U C Uᵀ. Inflate D for age, low SNR and footprint mismatch, including (distance × slope uncertainty)². A usable factor has measured or documented uncertainty and a finite age limit. With state predictor m and covariance P, minimize ||x−m||²_P + Σ 2ρ(whiten(y−h(x))) within independent blocks, where ||v||²_P = vᵀP⁻¹v, whiten uses the Cholesky factor of R and ρ is Huber with cutoff 1.5 standard deviations. Iterate weighted linear least squares using QR/SVD. Explicit latent shared errors with Gaussian penalties are an alternative for large blocks. Compare leave-one-family-out predictions, recording which uncertainty comes from shared parents. Check data-only singular values after scaling columns in physical units and removing nuisance parameters. A tight prior can stabilize a fit but cannot identify an unobserved contrast.

Implement the observation/ancestry schema and small linear factor solver once under #170 as pure functions. #171 and #180 supply ray derivatives, #172 consumes Jacobians and covariance, #173 and #174 can use the ancestry contract with their own path/template solvers. Fixtures use the same schema. Fit and replay in Linux Python 3.12 with **numpy==2.2.6, scipy==1.15.3**, single CPU thread for live benchmarks. Use OpenRVDAS standard Reader/Transform records. No mandatory third-party sonar SDK. Cost target for the shared dense step with n rows and p small state terms is O(np²+p³) for bounded-size covariance blocks, bounded by thinning/aggregating independent cells before whitening and marginalizing small shared blocks. A shared ping contributes once. For live use budget one core, 256 MiB state per module and p95 processing time below half the observed input interval on the target PC. Report gaps and queue pressure, preserve the acquisition feed. Raw water-column volumes, iterative broadband fits and whole-survey smoothers run offline.

**Output and evidence.** Every result includes estimate/unit or missing value, interval/uncertainty type, support, source IDs, event time, stale/excluded reasons, score, elapsed persistence and one sentence naming the discrepancy and first check. Default watch alarm requires 60 s above its threshold and clears after 60 s below 75% of it, using record time. A gap breaks continuity. Offline features use independent line/trace support as described below. A detected science feature has its own feature score. Its presence alone says nothing adverse about instrument QC.

Use GOOD (QARTOD 1), SUSPECT (3), FAULT_LIKELY (4), AMBIGUOUS (3), UNCORRECTABLE (4) and POSTHOC_REVIEW (2) as in reanalysis §10.1. CORRECTABLE is retrospective only and solely eligible to propose a #56 ledger layer. Certification of a correction needs independent Tier A holdout gain beyond its uncertainty, acceptable coverage and magnitude above twice its standard uncertainty. Store raw hashes, ancestry/independence, method, code SHA, config, interval, uncertainty and plot with the proposal. A named allowlisted reviewer approves or rolls back against a trusted checkpoint. Only the separately derived corrected field gets its own flag. Rerun from immutable raw plus approved layers. Novel profile modes, reflector picks and plume scores stay research products.

**Scoring contract.** Freeze fit, calibration and final test by cruise, site and whole survey line/station, with spatial buffers at least the footprint and estimated correlation length. Hide holdout IDs from the fitter and exclude sources with common ancestry from independent accuracy claims. Tier B is seeded injection with unseen final seeds. Tier C measures internal agreement. Separate blinded human science labels from Tier A calibration. Report baseline, support, misses including easy-case abstentions, false alarms, selective risk, latency and uncertainty width. Use paired bootstrap by line/site for gains. Check nominal 95% coverage against the desk's 90 to 98% band. Respect the reanalysis budgets of three holdout looks per sensor/cruise and five passes. Stop after two failed gains. Live claims require a causal, timed replay. All pass thresholds below are proposed pilot gates.

## #170 Quality-weighted depth | NOVEL

“MB and EK disagree. Which depth can I use?” Live/replay B/E/D/S/C at 1 Hz on a ship such as Revelle with concurrent heads. Use DEPTH_Consensus, DEPTH_LostBottom and DEPTH_Stale for context. Matched SBP TWT gives draft+cT/2 after trace-delay correction.

Fit y_it=d_t+b_i+U_i a_t+e_it with a local depth/rate predictor, calibration-centered slow bias priors and shared covariance UCUᵀ. On previous clean overlaps compute robust pair-difference means and squared MAD scales V_ij after detrending. Subtract shared-difference variance (U_i−U_j)C(U_i−U_j)ᵀ and mismatch variance. Fit V'_ij=v_i+v_j by nonnegative least squares, using effective independent block counts. Three independent families give v₁=(V'₁₂+V'₁₃−V'₂₃)/2. Connected overlaps identify relative offsets. Private variances require full rank of the pair-sum matrix. Use a zero-sum bias gauge until independently anchored. Freeze learning during alarms. A coarse grid has ≤10% of sonar precision after sonar agreement and cannot break a tie.

Use the backbone robust fit and uncertainty. Flag an absolute 10 s mean leave-family-out residual above 3 correlation-adjusted standard errors only if two independent other families agree within 2 SD. Missing geometry, two-family splits or common draft error limit attribution. A tech gets the residual and weight per family. Shore gets a bias history. CPU cost is one small fit/epoch. Compare to #141 median on unequal-noise, duplicate-channel, bank and +2 m step fixtures, then blocked check depths (V2/V3). Target ≥20% lower RMSE, ≥90% step recall and ≤1 false attribution/10 clean hours. Reject if a copied head tightens uncertainty.

## #181 Relative sediment attenuation tied to acoustic facies | NOVEL

“Is a deeper reflector weaker because the sediment absorbs sound or because the reflector itself changes?” Use archived S signed, calibrated or documented linear traces with bandwidth, pulse/gain history, and two #173 horizons in the same trace. Extract RMS band amplitude per trace in gated windows at native rate, then aggregate independent footprints. AGC or an envelope-only export permits a pick but disqualifies spectral inversion.

For near-normal paths let Δd_i=c_s ΔT_i/2 be layer thickness from independently measured sediment speed. For corrected upper/lower echo amplitudes define y_if=ln(A_lower/A_upper), with spreading and transmission uncertainty propagated. Fit y_if=b_patch,f−2 α_f Δd_i+ε_if, where α_f is one-way amplitude attenuation (Np/m). A within-patch difference cancels the unknown intercept b, which absorbs reflection/transmission ratios only if they are constant across the patch. Fit positive α by robust weighted regression after centering both axes within patch. Put half-normal priors on α and Gaussian penalties on second differences across equally spaced frequency bands. Assign patches using independently fixed MB facies or a blinded survey partition. Compare α on held-out thickness gradients. Allow variable b and report loss of identifiability in the sensitivity run. With no sediment speed report differential loss per unit TWT only. Impedance ratio Z₂/Z₁=(1+R)/(1−R) requires signed calibrated pressure reflection coefficient R, known transmission and near-normal geometry. Restrict absolute impedance to independent core/known-reflector anchors. Otherwise quote a relative reflection contrast.
