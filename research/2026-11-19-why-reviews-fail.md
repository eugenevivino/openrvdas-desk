# Why the reviews failed

**INFERRED. Yes, implementation quality was the largest reason these reviews held.** Of 196 blocking findings, 146 concerned behavior the brief required on realistic inputs or supported operating paths. After counting recurring findings once, that is **109 of 151 distinct findings, or 72%**. The other 42 distinct findings divide into 18 scope or design gaps and 24 hardening or reviewer-strictness findings. These reviews support a judgment about the held units. They do not establish the quality of the whole module portfolio.

## 4. Three process fixes

1. **INFERRED. Require a production-path regression and one variation that should preserve the answer.** For a delayed QC flag, deliver it before and after history eviction and compare the surviving last-good reading.
